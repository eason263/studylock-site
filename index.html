English

StudyLock keeps every app on a child's iPhone or iPad locked until they pass a short quiz based on the Singapore primary syllabus: maths for Primary 1–6, and English (Primary 1–6) and science (Primary 3–6) at standard difficulty; the easy and challenging levels are maths only. Most geometry and measurement, longer word problems, comprehension and composition aren't covered yet. A passed quiz opens the apps for the time the parent chose; then they lock again by themselves.

How does my child unlock the apps?

Open StudyLock and take the quiz. A locked app's screen can't open StudyLock directly (an iOS limit), so your child switches to StudyLock themselves; keeping it in the Dock helps. Getting 70% right opens all apps for the unlock length you set, up to what is left of your daily limit on quiz unlock time.

Why can't my child start a quiz?

After a failed quiz there is a short wait (a few minutes, longer after several fails in a row) before a new set. And once the day's quiz unlock time is used up, no new quiz is offered until midnight. You can change the daily limit, or open the apps yourself, in Parent settings.

How do I open the apps without a quiz?

Tap Parent in StudyLock, enter your PIN, and choose Open apps now. This doesn't use the daily quiz unlock time.

How do I turn StudyLock off?

Tap Parent, enter your PIN, and switch off Protection. Deleting StudyLock also removes every lock it set.

I forgot the parent PIN

Quick start mode: tap Forgot PIN? under the PIN pad, confirm with the device's passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and choose a new PIN. The reset is noted in Parent settings. If the device has no passcode, there is no reset: delete StudyLock and set it up again.

Child's device in Family Sharing: there is no reset on the device, because the child knows that device's passcode. Delete StudyLock and set it up again; if you set Deleting Apps to Don't Allow, allow it first in your child's Screen Time settings. The quiz history on the device is lost.

Why can my child still use some apps?

Apps you allowed in Parent settings stay available. Some system apps, such as Phone and Settings, can't be locked by Screen Time at all.

Can my child switch StudyLock off?

On a child's device in Family Sharing (recommended), no: removing StudyLock's Screen Time permission needs the parent, and deleting StudyLock is blocked as long as Deleting Apps is set to Don't Allow in your child's Screen Time settings, as setup asks. In quick start mode the parent PIN protects StudyLock's settings, but a child can turn off its Screen Time permission in Settings; StudyLock notes this the next time it is opened.

Can my child get around the lock?

Screen Time can't lock the Settings app, so a child who changes the date and time there can delay the re-lock. StudyLock locks the next time it is opened and, if the clock is still wrong then, notes it in Parent settings. If the device is off when an unlock ends, its apps may stay open for some time after it is back on; opening StudyLock locks them at once.

Does StudyLock collect my child's data?

No. It has no accounts, servers, ads or analytics, and it doesn't connect to the internet. Quiz history stays on the device. See the privacy policy.

简体中文

StudyLock 让孩子 iPhone 或 iPad 上的所有 App 保持锁定，直到孩子通过一组取材自新加坡小学教纲的小测验：数学 P1–P6，以及标准难度的英文（P1–P6）和科学（P3–P6）；「简单」和「挑战」难度只有数学。暂未覆盖大部分几何和度量、较长的应用题、阅读理解和作文。通过后，App 在家长设定的时间内开放，时间到了自动重新锁上。

孩子怎么解锁 App？

打开 StudyLock 做测验。被锁的 App 画面无法直接跳转到 StudyLock（iOS 的限制），孩子需要自己切换到 StudyLock，把它放在程序坞里会方便很多。答对 70% 就能在你设定的时长内打开所有 App，但不超过当天做题解锁的剩余时长。

为什么孩子不能开始做题？

没通过之后要等一会儿（几分钟，连续没通过会更久）才有新的一组题。另外，当天靠做题解锁的时长用完后，要到午夜才会再出题。你可以在家长设置里调整每日上限，或直接开放 App。

不做题怎么直接开放 App？

在 StudyLock 里点「家长」，输入 PIN，选择「立即开放 App」。这不占用每天靠做题解锁的时长。

怎么关闭 StudyLock？

点「家长」，输入 PIN，关掉「保护」。删除 StudyLock 也会解除它设置的所有锁定。

忘记了家长 PIN

快速开始模式：在 PIN 键盘下方点「忘记 PIN？」，用设备的密码、面容 ID 或触控 ID 确认，再设置新的 PIN。这次重设会记录在家长设置里。如果设备没有设置密码，就无法重设，请删除 StudyLock 后重新设置。

家庭共享中的孩子设备：设备上无法重设，因为孩子知道这台设备的密码。请删除 StudyLock 后重新设置；如果你把「删除 App」设成了「不允许」，要先在孩子的屏幕使用时间设置里允许。设备上的测验记录会丢失。

为什么孩子还能用某些 App？

你在家长设置里允许的 App 始终可用。另外，有些系统 App（例如电话和设置）无法被屏幕使用时间锁定。

孩子能关掉 StudyLock 吗？

家庭共享中的孩子设备（推荐）：不能。移除 StudyLock 的屏幕使用时间权限需要家长；只要按设置时的提示，在孩子的屏幕使用时间里把「删除 App」设成「不允许」，孩子也删不掉 StudyLock。快速开始模式：家长 PIN 保护 StudyLock 的设置，但孩子可以在「设置」里关掉它的屏幕使用时间权限，StudyLock 下次被打开时会记录下来。

孩子能绕过锁定吗？

屏幕使用时间无法锁定「设置」App，孩子如果在里面改日期和时间，可以推迟重新上锁。StudyLock 下次被打开时会立即锁定；如果当时时间还是错的，会记录在家长设置里。解锁到期时设备如果处于关机状态，开机后 App 可能还会开放一段时间；打开 StudyLock 会立刻上锁。

StudyLock 会收集孩子的数据吗？

不会。它没有账户、服务器、广告或统计分析，也不联网。测验记录只保存在设备上。详见隐私政策。

怎麼關閉 StudyLock？

為什麼孩子還能用某些 App？

你在家長設定裡允許的 App 一直可用。另外，有些系統 App（例如電話和設定）無法被螢幕使用時間鎖定。

孩子能關掉 StudyLock 嗎？

孩子能繞過鎖定嗎？

不會。它沒有帳號、伺服器、廣告或統計分析，也不連網。測驗記錄只保存在裝置上。詳見隱私權政策。
